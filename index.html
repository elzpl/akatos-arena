Who Is It For

Two Kinds of
Builders. Both Welcome.

First-Time Shippers

You have an idea, a half-built project, or nothing at all. The sprint is about crossing the basic milestones for the first time: pick a problem, build something, put it in front of a user, ask someone to pay for it.

Builders Getting Unstuck

You already have something in progress — a product with some users, or a project that hasn't broken through. The sprint is about finding revenue, growing an audience, and building momentum.
